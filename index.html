Growing a safer internet.

Hopebloom is being built around one simple idea: children deserve online spaces where they can explore, learn and grow without being exposed to harmful content.

Discover Hopebloom What we're building
Our mission

Childhood should have room to bloom.

The internet can be an incredible place for discovery. It can also expose young people to material they were never meant to encounter.

Hopebloom is being prepared as an online child-protection initiative focused on awareness, prevention, safer digital environments and practical resources.

Protect curiosity.
Preserve childhood.
What we care about

Protection starts before harm happens.

Our planned work focuses on reducing children's exposure to some of the most harmful areas of the internet.

◌

Graphic content

Helping young people avoid exposure to disturbing, violent and graphic material online.

◈

Sexual content

Supporting safer digital environments where children are protected from inappropriate sexual material.

+

Self-harm content

Working toward healthier online spaces that do not normalize or encourage harmful behavior.

Our philosophy

Technology should protect childhood, not take it away.

Hopebloom is being developed with prevention, education, responsible technology and child-centered thinking at its core.
